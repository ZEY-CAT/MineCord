MineCord Privacy Policy

This policy describes optional analytics on MineCord's public homepage and download page.

Optional Google Analytics

MineCord uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits to these pages when a visitor selects an age range of 18 or older and chooses Allow analytics. The age choice stays on the page. MineCord saves the analytics preference in this browser.

Google Analytics can use first-party cookies with a random browser identifier and process page views, basic browser and device information, and approximate location. Google uses IP addresses during collection and does not log or store individual IP addresses in GA4. MineCord configures the analytics cookies to expire after 30 days. Advertising consent and advertising personalization are disabled.

MineCord sends the public page address without query parameters. The analytics tag is absent from the signed-in app. MineCord does not send private messages, account identifiers, or passwords to Google Analytics.

Your choice

Analytics is off by default. You can decline, or open Analytics settings on either public page and select Turn off analytics to withdraw consent and remove the analytics cookies from that browser. Turning off analytics does not delete information already collected by Google. Visitors of all ages can use the public pages with analytics off.

Learn how Google processes this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
