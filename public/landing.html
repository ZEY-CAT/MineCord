Everything you need

MINECORD combines the best of Minecraft social features into one platform.

Servers & Channels

Create and join servers with text and voice channels. Invite friends with one-click codes. Full role and permission system.

Friends & DMs

Add friends, start direct messages, create group chats. See who's online and what they're playing.

WebRTC Voice Chat

Low-latency voice with TURN relay fallback. Push-to-talk, noise suppression, and per-user volume.

Secure & Private

End-to-end encrypted DMs, minisign-verified updates, no tracking. Your data stays yours.

Minecraft Integration

Link your Java/Bedrock account. Show in-game status. Eaglercraft browser connector support.

Cross-Platform

Web app, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android. Electron (full) and Tauri (lightweight) desktop builds.
